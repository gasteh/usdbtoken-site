About

What USDB is

USDB is an ERC‑20 token on Ethereum issued within the Blotix ecosystem. Blotix uses it to settle withdrawals of its platform clients: when a client withdraws, they receive USDB in their own wallet and are free to hold it, transfer it, or exchange it for USDT on Uniswap.

Its value is referenced to the US dollar and supported by reserves held by the issuer. No guarantee of price stability is made. Issuance and redemption are controlled by a multi‑signature Safe; the contract is not upgradeable and charges no transfer fees.

Testing phase. USDB is currently used internally to validate the system. It is not offered to the public, it is not an investment product, and it should not be purchased with the expectation of profit.
